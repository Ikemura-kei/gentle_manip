answer by 126 mm (leave-one-out). Tsai degrades sharply with small rotations:

Re-collect guidance: tilt the board 45-60 deg between captures, vary EE height much more
widely, and spread rotation axes (not just one rocking direction).

## Residual, and why it was NOT tuned away

-5.0 mm height and 1.67 deg tilt remain. Both are within the D435i's own absolute depth
accuracy (~1-2% of a 0.5-0.7 m range = 5-14 mm), and a range-dependent depth bias across an
obliquely-viewed plane produces exactly this signature (small height offset + small tilt).
Folding it into the extrinsic would bake SENSOR bias into a GEOMETRIC transform, so it was
left alone. If a residual bias later shows up in deployment, correct it explicitly via
`real_lab.yaml: point_cloud_shift` — which is what that knob is for.

**OPEN:** `point_cloud_shift: [0.009, 0.0, -0.0]` in real_lab.yaml is still the L515-era
value (a +9 mm x correction measured against the OLD camera at its OLD pose). It is not
valid for this camera and was deliberately left in place pending the user's decision.
