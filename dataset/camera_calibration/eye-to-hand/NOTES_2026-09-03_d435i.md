## Why a re-collect was ordered anyway

The pose set is weakly conditioned, which is WHY a single bad sample could swing the
answer by 126 mm (leave-one-out). Tsai degrades sharply with small rotations:

- median pairwise relative rotation **14.3 deg** (max 37.4); 22/55 pairs under 10 deg
- rotation-axis scatter anisotropy 4.8 (axes clustered, not spread)
- EE height spread only **5 cm** (z 0.095-0.144 m)
- residual table tilt 2.8 deg ~= +-12 mm of systematic height error across the workspace

Re-collect guidance: tilt the board 45-60 deg between captures, vary EE height much more
widely, and spread rotation axes (not just one rocking direction).
